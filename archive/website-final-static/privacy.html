1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Trading Titans ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Trading Titans app and website (the "Service"). We aim to collect only what we need to run a safe, fun simulation. Remember: Trading Titans is a virtual trading simulator and never handles your real money or real brokerage accounts.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details — such as your name or display name and email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • Support & community content — messages you send to support, and feature ideas you submit to the community wishlist.

Information created as you use the Service

  • Virtual activity — your virtual trades, orders, holdings, positions, watchlist, price alerts, and virtual wallet balance. This data is virtual and has no monetary value.
  • Device & technical data — app version, device type, and a push-notification token (if you enable notifications), used to deliver alerts and keep the app working.
  • Usage & diagnostics — basic logs, crash reports, and performance data that help us find and fix problems and improve the Service.

We do not collect your real bank account, real brokerage account, card numbers, or government IDs for trading, because no real trading takes place. Any optional in-app purchases are processed by your app store, not by us, and we do not receive your full payment-card details.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, secure, and operate your account and the simulation.
  • To run features such as portfolios, watchlists, price alerts, rewards, and referrals.
  • To send notifications you've opted into, such as order fills and price alerts.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To monitor for abuse, enforce rate limits, protect the Service, and keep it reliable.
  • To analyse usage in aggregate and improve the product.

4. Why we are allowed to process your data

Depending on your location, we rely on one or more of the following: performing our contract with you (providing the Service), our legitimate interests (keeping the Service safe and improving it), your consent (for example, push notifications), and compliance with legal obligations.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service — for example, cloud hosting and database infrastructure (Supabase) and push-notification and analytics/crash-reporting services (such as Google Firebase). They process data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.
  • Other users, only to the limited extent of features you choose to take part in, such as a referral you send to a friend.
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.

6. How we protect your data

Passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider using strong one-way hashing; we never see or store your raw password. Access to your data is restricted by row-level security so that you can only access your own records. We apply rate limiting on public endpoints and follow sensible security practices. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and continuously improve our safeguards.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. To keep the Service lean, some data is automatically tidied over time — for example, read notifications are removed after roughly 90 days, and older order history is archived and then deleted on a defined schedule. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain certain records to comply with the law or resolve disputes.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access, correct, or update your account information from within the app.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
  • Turn notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these, email us at support@thetradingtitans.com.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app where appropriate. Continued use of the Service means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@thetradingtitans.com.

This document is provided for general informational purposes and is a starting template. Privacy laws vary by region (for example, India's DPDP Act and the GDPR); we recommend having it reviewed by a qualified legal professional before public launch.
