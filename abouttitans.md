# About Trading Titans

## What is Trading Titans?

Trading Titans is a virtual stock trading app built for the Indian market. It lets
people experience the thrill of trading shares, futures, and options without
risking a single rupee of their own money. Every account runs on virtual cash, so
users can buy, sell, win, and lose in a realistic environment that mirrors the real
markets, all in a completely safe space.

Think of it as a flight simulator, but for the stock market. Beginners can learn the
ropes, and experienced traders can test strategies, all while competing with others
and tracking how they would have performed if it were real.

## The Motive

The core idea behind Trading Titans is simple: trading is intimidating and expensive
to learn the hard way. Most people lose money early because they are learning while
real money is on the line. Trading Titans removes that fear.

The app exists to:

- Help people learn how trading actually works through hands-on practice.
- Let users build confidence and test strategies before they ever touch real money.
- Make learning fun and competitive through leaderboards, rewards, and challenges.
- Give traders honest feedback on their decisions so they improve over time.

It is part learning tool, part game, and part personal trading journal, all rolled
into one experience designed to turn curious beginners into confident "Titans."

## How It Works

A user's journey through the app usually looks like this:

1. **Sign up and get started** - Create an account, log in, and receive a starting
   balance of virtual cash to trade with.

2. **Explore the markets** - Browse popular Indian stocks and watch live market
   movement through the "Market Pulse," which tracks major indices and tickers. Users
   can build a personal watchlist of the stocks they care about.

3. **Place trades** - Buy and sell stocks, and step into the world of derivatives with
   futures and options, including a full option chain to explore. Orders feel just like
   a real trading app, but the money is virtual.

4. **Manage a portfolio** - Track holdings, open positions, and overall profit and loss
   in real time. The dashboard shows total portfolio value at a glance.

5. **Use a virtual wallet** - Top up the practice balance through different "boost"
   packs when users want more virtual cash to play with. (Currently offered as part of
   a beta testing phase.)

6. **Sharpen skills with advanced tools**:
   - **What-If Simulator** - See how an investment would have turned out if it had been
     made at an earlier date.
   - **Autopsy** - A post-trade breakdown that reviews wins and losses so users can
     understand what went right or wrong.
   - **Analytics** - Deeper insights into trading performance and habits.

7. **Compete and earn**:
   - **Weekly League** - A leaderboard where traders are ranked against each other,
     turning practice into friendly competition.
   - **Refer & Earn** - Invite friends and both sides receive virtual cash rewards.
   - **Rewards** - Surprise bonuses and celebrations land directly in the wallet.

8. **Stay connected** - Get notifications for order fills, price alerts, and
   announcements, each with its own distinct sound. Users can also share feature ideas
   through a community wishlist and reach out through support tickets.

The app also gently guides new users with an interactive walkthrough, and keeps
everyone on the latest version with update prompts and a maintenance mode for smooth
upkeep.

## Look and Feel

Trading Titans carries a premium, "royal" identity that matches its name. The
branding leans into the idea of becoming a trading champion, anchored by a crown logo
and a clean, confident, modern style.

### Color Theme

The app is built around a polished **blue** palette that feels trustworthy and
financial, paired with crisp neutrals:

- **Primary brand blue** - A bright, imperial blue used for buttons, highlights, and
  key actions.
- **Deeper and lighter blues** - Used for accents, gradients, and supporting elements
  to add depth.
- **Backgrounds** - Soft, airy off-white and pale blue tones in light mode for a clean
  feel.
- **Text** - Deep navy and ink tones that are easy to read against the light surfaces.

The app fully supports both a **light mode** and a **dark mode**. In dark mode, the
soft whites are swapped for rich deep-navy backgrounds, keeping the same calm, premium
feel while being easier on the eyes at night.

To communicate trading outcomes clearly, the app uses familiar market colors:

- **Green** for gains and bullish movement.
- **Red** for losses and bearish movement.
- Occasional **blue and violet** accents for informational highlights.

### Fonts

The entire app uses **Inter**, a clean and modern sans-serif typeface. It is highly
legible at small sizes, which matters a lot when showing numbers, prices, and
percentages. The app uses a wide range of weights, from light body text to very bold
headlines, to create a clear visual hierarchy between titles, values, and supporting
details.

### Currency

All values are shown in **Indian Rupees (₹)**, reinforcing that the app is tailored
for Indian market traders.

## In Short

Trading Titans is a safe, fun, and competitive playground for learning how to trade
the Indian markets. It blends realistic trading, smart analysis tools, and game-like
rewards into one polished blue-themed experience, helping everyday people grow from
nervous beginners into confident market Titans, all without risking real money.
